Origin

Brewed on the 40th floor.

Crimson Cola started as a rooftop experiment: real cane sugar, a hit of blood orange, and just enough carbonation to keep pace with a city that never really lands. The can is built for one hand, because your other hand is usually busy holding onto something eighty stories up.

Every batch is cooled in the shade of a water tower and shipped down before sunrise, block by block, the same way it's always been done here.
